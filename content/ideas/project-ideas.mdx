## Idea Validation Framework

### Evaluation Criteria
When considering a new project idea, I evaluate it based on:

1. **Problem-Solution Fit**
   - Does it solve a real problem?
   - Is the solution valuable?

2. **Technical Feasibility**
   - What are the technical challenges?
   - Do I have the necessary skills?

3. **Market Potential**
   - Who would use this?
   - Is there a target audience?

4. **Personal Interest**
   - Am I passionate about this?
   - Will I maintain interest long-term?

### Project Triage
- **Backlog:** Ideas to evaluate later
- **Active:** Currently being developed
- **On Hold:** Temporarily paused
- **Completed:** Finished and deployed
